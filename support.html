Parking Spot Hero Support

How to use Parking Spot Hero and get help.

How to Save Your Car

When you park, open Parking Spot Hero and tap Save My Car. The app will attempt to determine your current location using your iPhone's location services.

Once a location is available, review the address and add any parking details you want to remember.

Parking Details

You can optionally record information such as:

  • Parking level or floor
  • Section
  • Row
  • Parking spot number
  • Notes
Tip: In a large parking garage, the level, section, row, and spot number may be more useful than GPS alone.

Parking Photos

You can attach a photo to your saved parking location. Photos are useful for remembering nearby signs, elevators, garage levels, landmarks, or other details that can help you find your vehicle later.

Parking Timer

If your parking location has a time limit, enable the parking timer before saving your car.

You can select one of the quick timer options or choose a custom expiration time. Parking Spot Hero can provide notifications as your parking expiration time approaches.

When 30 minutes or less remain, the parking expiration information is highlighted in red. After the timer expires, the app continues to display the parking expiration time.

What If GPS Is Unavailable?

Parking garages, underground structures, airports, and other enclosed areas can make it difficult for your iPhone to obtain an accurate GPS location.

Parking Spot Hero will continue trying to determine your location. If a GPS location cannot be obtained, the app will offer the option to enter the parking address or place manually.

You can enter a street address, airport, parking garage, venue, or other recognizable place and continue saving your parking information.

Tip: If you are deep inside a parking garage, save the garage or venue location and use the Level, Section, Row, Spot, Notes, and Photo fields to record exactly where the vehicle is parked.

Finding Your Car

After saving your parking location, return to the main screen and tap Find My Car.

Parking Spot Hero displays your location and your saved vehicle location on the map and helps you determine the direction and distance back to your vehicle.

When you reach your vehicle, use Found My Car to end the current parking session.

Open in Maps

Tap Open in Maps when you want full walking directions using your selected mapping application.

This is especially useful when your vehicle is farther away and you want turn-by-turn walking directions.

Location Accuracy

GPS accuracy varies depending on your surroundings. Outdoor locations with a clear view of the sky generally provide better results than underground garages or enclosed structures.

For difficult parking locations, use GPS together with parking details and a photo whenever possible.

Notifications Are Not Appearing

Make sure notifications are enabled for Parking Spot Hero in your iPhone settings. Parking reminders depend on notification permission being granted.

Location Is Not Working

Make sure Location Services are enabled for Parking Spot Hero in your iPhone settings. The app needs location access to automatically determine where your vehicle is parked and to assist you in returning to it.
