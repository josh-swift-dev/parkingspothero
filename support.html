Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save my parking location?

Open Parking Spot Hero and tap Quick Park to save your current GPS position. For additional information, choose Save With Details.

Can I save a spot without GPS?

Yes. Use the manual address option when your GPS signal is unavailable or inaccurate. You can also adjust the map pin to better represent your location.

How do I find my parked car?

Tap Find My Car to view your saved parking location and your position on the map. You can also select Open in Maps (Backup) to navigate using Apple Maps.

How do parking reminders work?

Set a parking timer while saving or editing a parking session. The app schedules a notification based on your selected time. Make sure notifications are enabled in your iPhone settings.

Can Parking Spot Hero detect parking automatically?

Yes. Optional automatic parking detection uses available location and motion information to suggest when you may have parked. This feature depends on device permissions, background settings, and signal quality. You can always save a spot manually.

Why is my GPS location inaccurate?

Parking garages, underground locations, tall buildings, and indoor environments can interfere with GPS accuracy. Adjust the saved pin or enter the location manually when needed.

Can I attach a photo to a parking spot?

Yes. Save a photo of your parking area, garage sign, floor number, or nearby landmark to help you recognize the location later.

What happens when I tap End Parking?

Your active parking session ends, the record is moved to parking history, and pending parking reminders for that session are canceled.

Does the app work without an internet connection?

Previously saved parking coordinates remain available on your device. Some features, including map imagery, address lookup, and navigation, may require internet access.

Does Parking Spot Hero require an account?

No. You can use the app's core parking features without creating an account.

Troubleshooting

Location Not Working?

  • Open your iPhone's Settings app.
  • Go to Privacy & Security → Location Services.
  • Select Parking Spot Hero.
  • Allow location access and enable Precise Location.
  • Try saving your parking location again.

Not Receiving Parking Reminders?

  • Open Settings → Notifications.
  • Select Parking Spot Hero.
  • Enable Allow Notifications.
  • Check that your parking timer has not expired.
  • Review Focus settings that may silence alerts.

Automatic Parking Detection Not Working?

  • Make sure automatic detection is enabled in the app.
  • Check location and motion permissions.
  • Allow background location access when requested.
  • Remember that detection is not guaranteed for every trip.

App Not Behaving as Expected?

  • Close and reopen Parking Spot Hero.
  • Make sure your iPhone is running a supported iOS version.
  • Check your device's location and notification settings.
  • Contact support if the issue continues.

Privacy & Your Data

Parking Spot Hero uses location information to save and locate your parked vehicle. Parking records and associated photos are stored locally on your device.

The app does not require an account for its core features. Location information may be passed to Apple Maps when you choose external navigation.

For complete information, please review the Privacy Policy available through the app.
