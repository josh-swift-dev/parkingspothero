Parking Spot Hero Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Parking Spot Hero is designed to help you remember where you parked your vehicle. Your privacy is important, and the app is designed to collect only the information needed to provide its parking-location features.

Location Information

Parking Spot Hero uses your device's location services to determine and save the location of your parked vehicle. Location information is used to display your parking location, help you return to your vehicle, and provide related location-based features.

Parking locations are stored on your device as part of the app's saved parking information. Parking Spot Hero does not operate its own server for collecting or tracking your location.

Manually Entered Locations

If GPS is unavailable, Parking Spot Hero may allow you to manually enter an address or place. This information is used to establish the parking location you choose to save.

Parking Details

You may optionally save information such as a parking level, section, row, parking spot number, notes, and parking expiration time. This information is used only to provide the app's parking reminder and vehicle-location features.

Photos

Parking Spot Hero may allow you to take or select a photo associated with a parking location. For example, you may choose to save a photo of a parking garage sign, level, row, or nearby landmark.

Photos associated with parking records are stored as part of the app's local parking information.

Notifications

If you enable parking timer reminders, Parking Spot Hero may send local notifications to remind you when your parking time is approaching expiration or has expired.

Data Collection

Parking Spot Hero does not require you to create an account. The app does not sell your personal information and does not operate an advertising or user-tracking system.

Third-Party Map Services

Parking Spot Hero may allow you to open a saved parking location in an external mapping or navigation application. When you choose to use an external service, your use of that service is subject to the privacy policy and terms of that provider.

Data Removal

You can remove saved parking information and parking history using the controls provided within the app. Removing the app from your device may also remove locally stored app data, subject to Apple's device backup and restoration features.

Children's Privacy

Parking Spot Hero is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support information provided for Parking Spot Hero.
