Parking Spot Hero

Privacy Policy | Updated October 11, 2026

Parking Spot Hero helps you save your parking location, navigate back to your vehicle, and manage parking reminders. We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the information needed to provide these features.

1. Information Used by the App

Depending on the features you choose to use, Parking Spot Hero may access:

  • Location: To save your parking position, display maps, and help you find your vehicle.
  • Motion and Fitness: If you enable automatic parking detection, motion information may help identify transitions between driving and walking.
  • Photos and Camera: To attach a parking photo when you choose to use this feature.
  • Notifications: To deliver parking expiration reminders when enabled.
  • Parking Details: Such as notes, parking levels, spot numbers, and saved locations.

2. Storage

Parking information is stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account or operate a developer cloud account for storing your parking records.

Information may be shared with another application, such as Apple Maps, when you explicitly use a feature that opens or shares a parking location.

3. Location Permissions

Location access is used to support parking-related features. Automatic parking detection may require additional location permissions, including background access, depending on the feature and iOS settings.

You can manage location permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Purchases

Parking Spot Hero offers an optional one-time Pro purchase. Payments and purchase verification are handled through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

The developer does not receive your complete payment card information. Apple may process purchase information according to its own privacy policy.

5. Analytics and Advertising

Parking Spot Hero does not integrate RevenueCat for purchase processing. This policy does not claim that Apple or iOS collect no diagnostics or App Store-related information.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your saved parking information. Location details may be passed to mapping services when you request navigation or sharing.

7. Data Removal

You can end or remove parking records using available app controls. Removing the app may also remove locally stored parking information, subject to iOS backup and restoration behavior.

8. Children's Privacy

Parking Spot Hero is not designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as features change. Updates will be posted on this page.
